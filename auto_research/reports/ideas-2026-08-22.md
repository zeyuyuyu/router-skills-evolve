## Top 2 Relevant Papers

### 1. Task- and Session-Level Model Routing (arxiv:2608.14641) — August 2026
**Relevance: HIGH — directly supports camera-ready §2 router positioning**

Common-interface hybrid evaluation of four open-source LLM routers (MatryoshkaRouter, Aurelio, vLLM Semantic Router, plus one commercial) across RouterBench, BFCL v4, tau2-bench, and WebArena (290 tasks × 2,610 candidate outcomes). Central finding: **three of four routers emit near-constant tier assignments** — they do not vary meaningfully with prompt content. Always-Mid (constant-assignment baseline) matches Aurelio on three benchmarks. Only vLLM Semantic Router varies with prompt, but achieves lowest success rate on all benchmarks.

Significance for MERA camera-ready §2: establishes that routing *variability* — not just routing infrastructure — is the unsolved problem. MERA's router, trained on per-task oracle labels (SCALING_FORCE_BOTH=1), produces a prompt-sensitive routing signal. **EXP-183** (offline, ~1h) audits MERA cycle-3 router variability: probability variance, non-constant fraction, routing entropy vs. constant baselines. ### 2. Latent Thought Credit: Multi-Answer Credit Assignment (arxiv:2608.01593) — August 2026
**Relevance: MEDIUM-HIGH — addresses MERA's zero-variance GRPO group problem**

For standard GRPO, when K/K rollouts succeed or 0/K succeed, group standard deviation → 0 → zero advantage → no gradient. Latent Thought Credit replaces the scalar reward with a latent-space proximity signal: extract final-token latent representations, compute cosine similarity to the correct-answer manifold, use blended advantage = α·(reward − mean)/std + (1−α)·latent_credit. Fallback for zero-variance groups: use latent_credit directly. Tested on math reasoning (Qwen2.5-7B, GSM8K/MATH): +3.1% pass@1 over standard GRPO, primarily from recovery on easy (all-correct) and hard (all-fail) groups.

MERA connection: cycle-3 GRPO Phase 3b ACR=52.4% (47.6% of groups contribute zero gradient). **EXP-184** applies this with α annealed from 0.5→1.0, latent layer −1 of Qwen3-4B, fallback to direct latent credit for zero-variance groups. GPU needed; queued for restoration. Priority 6.
