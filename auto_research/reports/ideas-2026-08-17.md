### 1. arxiv:2608.01837 + 2608.05987 — 4-Paper Privileged Distillation Cluster Completion

**Relevance: ★★★ — CLUSTER COMPLETE — §2 MUST COVER ALL FOUR — CAMERA-READY CRITICAL**

**PCSD (2608.01837, Persistent Consistency for Self-Distillation in Agentic RL)**

Derives token-level distillation weights from the local persistence of teacher-favoring signals over a sliding window. Three components: (1) adaptive window aggregation with exponential decay captures persistent relative teacher support (long-range signal); (2) trend-aware modulation attenuates locally declining support (prevents stale corrections); (3) sigmoid gating produces continuous weights jointly optimized with GRPO. Effect: GRPO loss term is reweighted by the token-level certainty that the teacher was consistently better at this decision point.

Results: +15.6pp / +13.3pp over GRPO on ALFWorld (two backbones); competitive on WebShop; +15.8pp on unseen ALFWorld split. Best ALFWorld Overall among all baselines.

**AgentOPSD (2608.05987, Recursive Self-Distillation for Agentic RL)**

Aggregates token-level teacher-student log-probability gaps into turn-level evidence by recursively updating a Bayesian belief state in log-odds space. Key insight: marginal belief revision identifies *pivotal turns* — turns where the teacher's incremental information shifts the belief state most. Distillation focuses on these pivotal turns, avoiding dilution from non-pivotal interactions. Results: 89.1% success on ALFWorld with Qwen2.5-7B, surpassing GRPO and all prior self-distillation baselines.

**Cluster summary (4 papers):**

| Paper | Mechanism | Privileged Signal | Training-only? | ALFWorld Result |

### 2. arxiv:2608.06296 — "On-Policy Self-Distillation without Any Supervision" (u-OPSD)

**Relevance: ★★★ — NEW PAPER — 5th SELF-DISTILLATION CLUSTER MEMBER — DISTINCT UNSUPERVISED ANGLE**

**Key finding** (August 2026): u-OPSD achieves on-policy self-distillation with NO external supervision — no ground truth labels, no environmental feedback, no teacher model. Method: (1) sample K rollouts from the policy; (2) construct a pseudo-solution via majority vote under a self-consistency threshold; (3) condition the model on the pseudo-solution and distill on disagreeing completions, correcting precisely where the model is *confidently wrong* (high-consistency pseudo-solution, low-consistency policy response). Results: consistent improvement over base models across diverse benchmarks; matches or surpasses supervised distillation approaches.

**MERA's two-regime relationship to u-OPSD:**

| Cycle | Supervision | u-OPSD analog | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cycle 0 | External GPT-5.5 teacher traces | NONE (MERA uses supervised oracle) | MERA's oracle investment: external teacher yields highest-quality initial procedure (1102 chars); u-OPSD would use only own rollouts |
| Cycles 1–3 | Small model's own successful traces collected by router | Majority-vote pseudo-solution from own rollouts | Router selects tasks where small model passes@1 → these are MERA's self-consistency successes; SkillBook updates from these ≡ distilling on consistent successes |
| Collapse (c1–c3) | SkillBook procedure 17–21 words (EXP-163) | Correction rate → 0 as self-consistency improves | u-OPSD predicts: as model self-consistently solves more tasks, correction signal shrinks; MERA's collapse is consistent with this prediction |

**Camera-ready §2 addition:**

u-OPSD (arxiv:2608.06296) extends the self-distillation paradigm to the fully unsupervised
