## Top 3 Relevant Papers

### 1. GRPO is Secretly a Process Reward Model
**arxiv:2509.21154** | Sullivan & Koller | Sep 2025 / revised May 2026

Proves that GRPO equipped with an outcome reward model is equivalent to a PRM-aware RL
objective with an implicit Monte-Carlo-based process reward, given that rollout subsets
share identical prefixes. The paper also identifies a flaw in the GRPO objective when
steps are imbalanced and proposes λ-GRPO as a mitigation, showing improvements on
downstream reasoning tasks.

**MERA intersection:** MERA §3 already cites arxiv:2507.05386 for the 52.4% zero-variance
DAPO groups finding. arxiv:2509.21154 adds a principled PRM-framing: zero-variance groups
are exactly the groups where the implicit PRM assigns uniform step credit. This deepens
the mechanistic explanation without new experiments. λ-GRPO is a sibling of Dr.GRPO
(EXP-120) and DAPO — one sentence in §7 Remedies closes the design-space coverage gap.

**Not yet in paper v13.** → EXP-210.

### 2. Beyond Top-k Skill Retrieval: Diversity-Aware Skill Routing for LLM Agents
**arxiv:2609.05824** | September 2026

Proposes Diverse Skill Routing (DSR), a Determinantal Point Process reranking framework
over large skill registries for LLM agents. The query-residual diversity kernel penalizes
redundant skill overlap while maintaining relevance to the query. On the SkillRouter
benchmark, DSR improves recall and full coverage over strong pointwise baselines, with
larger gains on multi-skill queries.

**MERA intersection:** MERA's SkillBook intentionally uses a single global "coding" skill
(CLAUDE.md Decision #1), sidestepping multi-skill retrieval entirely. DSR optimizes *which
skills* to combine (diversity within a skill set); MERA optimizes *which model* executes
a (single) skill (routing decision). These are complementary axes. A §2 paragraph
positioning DSR vs. MERA will close what ICLR reviewers will likely flag as a gap —
this is a Sep 2026 paper directly at the intersection of skill routing and LLM agents.

**Not yet in paper v13.** → EXP-211.
