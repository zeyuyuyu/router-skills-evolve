## Top 3 Relevant Papers Found Today

### 1. arxiv:2606.18487 — "SFT Overtraining Predicts Rank Inversion via Entropy Collapse Under RLVR"

**Relevance: ★★★ — HIGHEST PRIORITY TODAY**

**Key result**: Selecting the highest-eval-score SFT checkpoint for GRPO initialization is counterproductive. In controlled experiments across Qwen-2.5 3B and 7B on HumanEval, selecting the highest-scoring checkpoint (often at 5–6 training epochs) consistently produces the WORST GRPO initializer: lower GRPO peak pass@10 by 0.325 margin vs the epoch-1 checkpoint.

**Mechanism**: SFT overtraining compresses the output distribution (entropy collapse → low token diversity). GRPO relies on within-group variance for gradient signal. Starting from an entropy-collapsed checkpoint → near-zero gradient at GRPO step 1 → gradient vanishing → slow or absent GRPO improvement.

**Direct application to our pipeline**: Our cycle-1 SFT shows the smoking gun:
- Epoch-1 loss = 0.166, epoch-2 loss = 0.271 (loss INCREASES — overtrained)
- Epoch-1 entropy = 0.255, epoch-2 entropy = 0.207 (compressed by overtraining)
- The GRPO-1 phase was initialized from checkpoint-12 (epoch 2), which is the overtrained state
- This explains the cycle-1 skills arm dip (65.85% vs 70.73%)

**Fix**: Auto-select the epoch-1 checkpoint when `epoch2_loss > epoch1_loss`. One-line change to `run_full_pipeline.sh`. EXP-137 tests this fix.

**Paper impact**: §5.4 new Hypothesis F ("Cycle-1 Non-Monotonic Dip as SFT Epoch-2 Overfitting"), adds loss-reversal table (four-row comparison above) as evidence. Closes an unexplained observation that reviewers would flag as a soundness gap.

### 2. arxiv:2605.09608 — "Geometry Conflict: Explaining and Controlling Forgetting in LLM Continual Post-Training"

**Relevance: ★★★ — THEORETICAL GROUNDING FOR EXP-136 FINDING**

Proposes that catastrophic forgetting in LLM continual post-training is caused by **conflicting gradient geometries**: the gradient direction of new-task data (cycle N+1) conflicts with the parameter landscape of the prior model (trained on cycle N data). Measured by cosine similarity between new-task gradients and the gradient direction from the old-task minimum.

**Direct application**: Our cycle-1 SFT starts from the GRPO-0 adapter, which has been optimized to output specific solutions for specific HumanEval tasks (mode-collapsed). The cycle-1 SFT training data (new teacher traces, different tasks emphasis) has gradient directions that conflict with the GRPO-0 attractor. Epoch 1 of SFT overcomes this conflict partially (loss drops). Epoch 2 fails: gradient conflict "snaps" the parameters back toward the GRPO-0 mode, causing loss to INCREASE to 0.271.

**Distinction from Hypothesis D and E**: 
- Hypothesis D (GRPO forgetting SFT): SFT → GRPO phase causes forgetting (via std-norm amplified gradients)
- Hypothesis E (rise-and-collapse): Within GRPO training, performance peaks then falls
- **Hypothesis F (new today)**: SFT epoch-2 overfitting from geometry conflict with prior GRPO adapter → overtrained SFT checkpoint → poor GRPO initialization → non-monotonic skills arm dip

This adds a new, SFT-phase causal pathway to the existing Hypothesis D/E analysis.

**Paper impact**: §5.4 Hypothesis F paragraph; Figure showing loss trajectories across cycles with cycle-1 flagged as geometric-conflict victim.
