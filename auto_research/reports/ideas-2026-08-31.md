## Top 3 Relevant Papers

### 1. Prefix Teach, Suffix Fade: Local Teachability Collapse in Strong-to-Weak On-Policy Distillation
**arxiv:2605.13643** | May 2026

Proves that in strong-to-weak on-policy distillation the teacher's gradient signal
is concentrated in early tokens (prefix zone) and degrades for later tokens (suffix
fade). Directly maps to MERA's SFT format `f"{procedure}\n\n---\n\n{problem}"`: the
constant procedure prefix occupies the high-teachability prefix zone but carries zero
novel information for the student (teacher and student already agree on it). The
actual skill transfer — the solution the student must learn — is in the suffix zone
where teachability collapses. This provides a principled 2026 theoretical explanation
for Hypothesis F: cycle-1 is the first cycle with a non-trivial procedure prefix, so
the student saturates procedure-prefix loss quickly, then over-updates on the solution
suffix, causing the epoch-2 loss spike (+0.105 overfit gap). Cycles 2-3 avoid the
spike because the model already memorized the prefix.

**ICLR 2027 relevance:** Strengthens §2b on-policy distillation with a May 2026
citation; gives Hypothesis F a principled theoretical name beyond "geometry conflict".

### 2. RL Forgets! Towards Continual Policy Optimization
**arxiv:2607.04364** | July 2026

Analyzes continual RL settings where a model is trained across multiple sequential
RL episodes, debating whether GRPO's KL regularization term is sufficient to prevent
catastrophic forgetting. Shenfeld et al. 2026 argue KL directly reflects forgetting;
Lai et al. 2025 and Chen et al. 2025a argue it is not the main factor. MERA's
SFT→GRPO alternation addresses this via a stronger mechanism: GRPO always
re-initializes from the latest SFT checkpoint (Design Decision #6), making the SFT
checkpoint the GRPO reference model rather than an increasingly-stale base. The
4-cycle trajectory (no RL-induced forgetting spikes; cycle-1 dip is SFT-caused) is
direct supporting evidence.

**ICLR 2027 relevance:** Closes the §7 Remedies gap — currently discusses SFT-overfit
forgetting (Hypothesis F) but not RL forgetting across cycles. Adds a 2026 continual
RL citation that frames MERA's checkpoint boundary as a deliberate design.

### 3. A Formula-Driven Survey and Research Agenda for On-Policy Distillation
**arxiv:2606.22793** | June 2026

Comprehensive survey formalizing the on-policy distillation design space, including
variants that use run-both oracle traces (teacher + student evaluated on the same
inputs). MERA's run-both oracle with `SCALING_FORCE_BOTH=1` is the canonical OPD
setup this survey frames. Useful as a structural anchor for §2b if ICLR reviewers
ask for more systematic related-work coverage.
