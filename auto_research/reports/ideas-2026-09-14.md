## Top 3 Relevant Papers

### 1. arxiv:2609.12578 — "From Collaboration to Capability: Internalizing Routed LLM Experts into Compact Reasoners" (Sep 11, 2026)

**Authors:** Frank Nie, Shuyao Wang, Ethan B. Liu

**Summary:** RIVET, a two-stage framework for compact model expert internalization:
- Stage I: expert-augmented RL — compact model routes to a stronger LLM expert and receives a shared outcome reward on both its routing decisions and the expert's returned solution spans.
- Stage II: verified trajectory SFT — successful complete interaction traces (compact + expert) are distilled into the compact model's weights via SFT.

**Results:** RIVET-1.7B and RIVET-4B reach 28.25% and 44.16% mean accuracy across 7 competition-math benchmarks. Stage II improves RIVET-4B by +6.49pp after expert removal.

**Relevance to MERA:** RIVET is the closest published comparator to MERA's Phase 3a (SFT) + Phase 3b (GRPO) evolution mechanism. Both systems implement route-to-expert → collect verified traces → train student → student handles tasks without expert. Key MERA differentiators: (1) multi-cycle joint co-evolution with a re-calibrating router, (2) SkillBook procedural conditioning, (3) SFT-first ordering (vs. RIVET's RL-first). **Not yet cited in MERA §2 or §5.**

### 2. arxiv:2609.09153 — "Procedural Graphs: Self-Evolving Execution Structures for LLM Agents" (Sep 8, 2026)

**Authors:** Yuxing Lu, Yicheng Chen, Shanchan Wu, Sercan Ö. Arık

**Summary:** Procedural Graph organizes procedural knowledge for LLM agents as (procedure, relation, procedure) triplets — graph-structured analogue to MERA's flat SkillBook text. At each decision step, the agent localizes its active graph node and receives subgraph-derived situational guidance biasing (but not dictating) next actions. Self-evolves from agent interaction traces by adding new procedure nodes/edges.

**Relevance to MERA:** Direct structural comparator to MERA's §3 SkillBook. Both address the same problem (encoding what-to-do procedural knowledge to guide execution without weight updates), but with opposite design choices: Procedural Graph = typed graph topology, MERA = flat text, single global skill. MERA's flat-vs-graph design rationale is not currently articulated in §3. **Not yet cited.**
