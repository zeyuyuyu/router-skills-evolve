### 1. arxiv:2608.24747 — SkillForge: Evolving Verifiable Skills for Reinforcement Learning Agents (NEW — submitted 2026-08-25, 3 days ago)

**Authors:** Shidong Yang, Ziyu Ma et al. (Alibaba/AMAP)

**Key finding:** SkillForge proposes continuous skill evolution for RL agents via two mechanisms: (1) **evidence-based skill verification** — skills are tested against environment interaction and discarded/refined when evidence contradicts them; (2) **multi-pathway skill induction** — the skill bank grows by associating distinct trajectory types with distinct skills. Most RL-trained agents are episodic and cannot accumulate reusable knowledge; SkillForge enables cross-episode skill accumulation with quality control.

**Relevance to EXP-196:** MERA's SkillBook is architecturally parallel to SkillForge-Evolve: per-cycle procedure distillation ≡ evidence-based induction; pytest oracle ≡ environment verification; single global procedure ≡ single-pathway induction. The critical differentiator — MERA's Design Rule #1 single global skill — can now be justified via SkillForge-Evolve's framework: in homogeneous coding domains (HumanEval), multi-pathway induction collapses to a dominant pathway, validating the global approximation. ### 2. arxiv:2607.28048 — SKILL-KD: Contrastive Skill Distillation for LLM Agents (July 2026)

**Authors:** Qiming Shi, Yibo Dou, Jiawen Zhu et al.

**Key finding:** Standard skill acquisition from the student's failed trajectory or the teacher's trajectory alone both underperform. SKILL-KD explicitly contrasts teacher (success) vs. student (failure) trajectories to extract the "behavioral gap" — the actionable strategic difference that makes the teacher succeed where the student fails. Across five task families (QA, spreadsheet, document QA, math, embodied), SKILL-KD outperforms trajectory-only distillation.

**Relevance to EXP-197:** MERA's SCALING_FORCE_BOTH=1 run-both oracle produces exactly SKILL-KD's input format: per-task {teacher_pass, student_pass} flags. The behavioral gap (teacher_pass ∩ student_fail) ≈ 20.73% of train tasks (96.34% - 75.61%). MERA's SkillBook procedure is distilled from GPT-5.5 traces, which are implicitly concentrated on this gap population. Quantifying this provides a §2 camera-ready citation and §3.2 rationale for why oracle-based trace selection is contrastive-equivalent to SKILL-KD.
