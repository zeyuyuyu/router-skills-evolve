### EXP-187 (Priority 8) — CoDistill-GRPO: Co-Distillation Reward Signal Audit for MERA Phase 3b

**Paper:** arxiv:2604.02288 "Unifying Group-Relative and Self-Distillation Policy Optimization via Sample Routing"

**Key finding:** CoDistill-GRPO co-trains a large and small model: the small model receives a KD reward (log-ratio of large model's distribution) as dense supervision on top of GRPO's sparse outcome reward; the large model trains on the small model's rollouts with importance reweighting. The coupling creates a synergistic loop analogous to MERA's collect_traces → SFT → GRPO → router cycle.

**Relevance to MERA:** MERA's Phase 3b (GRPO on small model) currently uses only outcome reward (pass@1 on HumanEval tests). CoDistill-GRPO suggests adding a KD reward from the large model's log-probabilities on the rollouts collected during Phase 1. This is architecturally compatible with MERA's run-both oracle (Phase 1 already runs large+small on every task when `SCALING_FORCE_BOTH=1`). Offline audit: compute the KD reward signal retroactively on existing Phase-1 traces (MERA results/) and estimate whether it would have changed routing decisions or advantage weights.
