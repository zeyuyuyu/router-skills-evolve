### EXP-2: `grpo_curriculum_continual` — Hard-first curriculum GRPO from cycle-3 checkpoint
**ID:** `exp_2026_08_19_002_grpo_curriculum_hard_first`  
**Priority:** 8  
**Kind:** `grpo_curriculum_continual`

**Rationale:** arXiv 2605.00433 (RECRL) shows that adaptive curriculum sampling — presenting harder requirements first and adjusting difficulty based on per-task pass rates — improves data utilization during RL. Our cycle-3 data shows the small model passes 75.6% of training tasks but only 62.2% of test tasks; easy tasks dominate the training distribution and produce zero-advantage groups. Starting from the cycle-3 checkpoint, sorting HumanEval by per-task pass@1 (hardest first) should push the model on its failure tail. Expected runtime: ~2h on A800.

### EXP-4: `grpo_multi_seed_staircase` — 3-seed staircase on cycle-3 router with tighter cost target
**ID:** `exp_2026_08_19_004_grpo_staircase_3seeds_c3_costtarget25`  
**Priority:** 7  
**Kind:** `grpo_multi_seed_staircase`

**Rationale:** Cycle 3 cost is at 27.56% vs large — we want to test whether pushing the router threshold from 0.5 to 0.4 (accepting more false-positive routing to large) with multi-seed staircase GRPO training can reduce cost below 25% while keeping task_pass ≥ 90%. This stress-tests whether curriculum scope affects GRPO convergence stability, motivated by observations in arXiv 2605.00433.
