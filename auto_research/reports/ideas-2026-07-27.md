## Top 3 Relevant Papers Found Today

### 1. arxiv:2607.21273 — "The Dark Room in the Reward Channel: Dense Prediction Rewards Collapse GRPO-Trained LLM Agents — and What Actually Works"

**Relevance: ★★★ — HIGHEST PRIORITY TODAY**

GRPO's advantage formula is `A_i = (r_i − mean) / std`. In zero-variance groups (std → 0),
this divides by ε, amplifying tiny numerical differences in reward to O(1) advantages.
The "Dark Room" paper demonstrates this causes a degenerate absorbing state: the optimizer
maximizes a vacuous per-step prediction reward at the cost of task success.

**Direct impact on our pipeline**: Our 52.4% ACR groups are assumed to contribute *zero* gradient.
But if `std(r_group)` is numerically near-zero (not bit-for-bit zero), GRPO z-scoring makes
them contribute *amplified noise gradient* — consistent with Hypothesis D (GRPO forgets SFT
gains during Phase 3b).

**Fix**: Replace `advantages = (r − mean) / (std + ε)` with `advantages = r − mean`.
Zero-variance groups then contribute *exactly* zero gradient (not amplified). One-line change.

**Paper result**: On ALFWorld with Qwen3 models (1.7B–8B), this single change eliminates
collapse. No other modification needed.

**Motivates**: EXP-134 (offline diagnostic confirming near-zero std in our ACR groups) and
EXP-135 (2h GPU run with no-std-norm GRPO, cycle 3 starting point).

### 2. arxiv:2607.01763 — "Denser ≠ Better: Limits of On-Policy Self-Distillation for Continual Post-Training"

**Relevance: ★★★ — WARNING for EXP-128 (SC-SDPO)**

Self-Distillation Policy Optimization (SDPO) augments GRPO with dense token-level KL
toward the model's own greedy rollout. In single-cycle training, this accelerates
specialization. In **continual post-training** (multiple cycles), the paper shows:
- SDPO causes stronger forgetting than vanilla GRPO.
- A self-reinforcing teacher–student loop amplifies formatting artifacts across cycles.
- GRPO (sparse rewards only) is more conservative and better preserves cross-cycle capability.

**Impact on EXP-128** (SC-SDPO, pass-rate weighted SFT, already queued):
SC-SDPO's pass-rate weighting ŵ = √[p̂(1−p̂)] is derived from the current model's
own pass-rates — a self-conditioned signal that evolves across cycles. This is precisely
the self-reinforcing loop the paper warns about. Risk: SC-SDPO may cause MORE forgetting
than uniform SFT, worsening the non-monotonic cycle-1 skills arm dip.

**Recommendation**: EXP-134 (offline diagnostic) should include a SC-SDPO forgetting check
before EXP-128 is scheduled on A800. If easy-task regression is detected, EXP-128 should
be modified (cap ŵ_max = 0.5, or use linear ŵ instead of √[p̂(1−p̂)]).

### 3. arxiv:2607.16244 — "CIGPO: Contextual Information-Gain Policy Optimization for Multi-Turn Evidence-Reading LLM Agents"

**Relevance: ★★ — Orthogonal ACR fix via variance injection**

CIGPO addresses zero-advantage lock-in in multi-turn settings by assigning per-turn
intermediate rewards (information-gain proxy), preventing the group reward distribution
from collapsing to a constant. Removing GRPO's std normalization (EXP-135) achieves
a complementary fix from the advantage-computation side; CIGPO achieves it from the
reward-design side. For single-turn code generation (HumanEval), CIGPO's multi-turn
approach is less directly applicable, but the variance-injection idea motivates a simpler
version: add a small bonus reward for syntactically valid partial solutions (compiles
but doesn't pass all tests), breaking the all-fail group degeneracy.

**Action**: Not queued today (queue backlog = 143). Record for future consideration if
EXP-135 (no-std-norm) does not sufficiently close the Full=Router gap.

**Rationale**: The "Dark Room" paper proves that GRPO std normalization converts near-zero
std into O(1) spurious gradient. Our 52.4% ACR groups are described as "zero-gradient,"
but this assumes std is exactly zero — not near-zero. This offline analysis loads
`grpo_info.json` group reward arrays from all 4 cycles and computes the empirical
`std(r_group)` distribution. Classifies each group as:
- **Exactly-zero** (std < 1e-9): truly zero gradient under std-norm.
- **Near-zero** (std ∈ [1e-9, 1e-2]): amplified to high-magnitude spurious gradient.
- **Informative** (std > 1e-2): normal GRPO signal.

If ≥5% of groups are "near-zero," the Dark Room mechanism is active and EXP-135 becomes
AAAI-critical. Output: `dark_room_variance_report.json` with per-cycle statistics and
a `dark_room_risk_active` boolean.
