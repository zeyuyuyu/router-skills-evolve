## Top 3 Relevant Papers

### 1. arxiv:2602.08813 — FRPO: Fine-tuning Robust Policy Optimization

**"Robust Policy Optimization to Prevent Catastrophic Forgetting"**
Sabbaghi, Pappas, Javanmard, Hassani (UPenn / USC, Feb 2026)

Proposes FRPO: a max-min policy objective that optimizes the worst-case reward within
a KL ball of radius ε around the current policy. This forces GRPO to converge to a
**flat reward landscape** (not a sharp peak), so downstream SFT perturbations cannot
easily undo the GRPO gains. Implementation: `A_i ← A_i × exp(ε × A_i)` — 3 lines,
zero memory overhead. Results: substantially reduces safety degradation under subsequent
fine-tuning across multiple base models. Key relevance: our 4-cycle pipeline applies
SFT→GRPO→SFT→GRPO each cycle; FRPO directly addresses the GRPO→SFT forgetting direction
(new Hypothesis E), orthogonal to CPO-PMP (EXP-130, which addresses SFT→GRPO direction).

### 2. arxiv:2605.07689 — Gradient Starvation in Binary-Reward GRPO

**"Gradient Starvation in Binary-Reward GRPO: Why Group-Mean Centering Fails and
Why the Simplest Fix Works"** (Anonymous, May 2026)

Identifies that GRPO's within-group mean baseline is a noisy estimator for binary
rewards (K=8 samples → baseline variance = p(1-p)/8 ≈ 0.03). Proposes Running-Mean
Baseline (RMB): replace within-group mean with an EMA of rewards across recent batches
(β=0.9), smoothing over ~6,560 samples → 30× lower baseline variance. Results: +2.8
pass@1 HumanEval, +1.9 MBPP for 1.5B code model; 3.1× gradient variance reduction in
non-ACR groups. Directly relevant to our non-ACR groups (47.6% = all GRPO signal).

### 3. arxiv:2607.21273 — The Dark Room in the Reward Channel _(already queued as EXP-135)_

**"The Dark Room in the Reward Channel: Dense Prediction Rewards Collapse
GRPO-Trained LLM Agents — and What Actually Works"** (July 2026)

Demonstrates that GRPO's std normalization amplifies near-zero within-group variance
to O(1) spurious gradient — ACR groups may be actively harming training, not merely
contributing zero gradient. Fix: remove std division. Already queued as EXP-134
(offline diagnostic) and EXP-135 (no-std-norm GPU run). Included here as corroborating
evidence that combines with today's FRPO + RMB proposals to form a comprehensive
advantage-computation reform strategy.
